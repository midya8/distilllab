Choose the update

Teacher targets are fixed with respect to student parameters. Changing T regenerates both soft distributions. Prediction uses temperature 1.

What this cannot tell you

A negative directional derivative is a local first-order statement, not a finite-step decrease guarantee. Shared nonlinear parameters can make another row worse, and even the chosen row can overshoot at a large step. One step does not reproduce an epoch or establish generalization.

The new inspector uses stable, unclipped logit-space CE/KL. The original experiment's displayed loss helpers clamp probabilities at 1e-12; their formulas and results are retained unchanged. Below that floor the old clipped loss is not the function whose derivative the original gradient formula represents. This inspector explicitly audits the unclipped objective instead.

Binary64 exp/tanh may underflow or saturate. Near-zero derivatives and ties are numerical, not exact-real certificates. No calibration, causal mechanism, optimal temperature or production-scale gain is claimed.
